The problem

A cherished game with no easy place to play

A traditional Kuwaiti game is part of everyday culture, but for many people the only way to play it is in person. A new generation of entrepreneurs wanted to bring it online so that players could enjoy it anywhere. They needed to do it at the lowest possible cost, and they needed to be live quickly enough to test their business idea while it was still fresh.

How we solved it

Turnkey delivery, from first requirement to launch

The client arrived with an idea. Perennia took it from requirements through to a live product, so the client had one partner accountable for the whole project rather than several contractors.

  • A web portal built on a full-stack JavaScript stack, keeping the codebase simple to run and extend.
  • Payment gateways integrated so that players can pay directly within the portal.
  • A clean, browsable layout that lets players find a game and start playing within a few clicks.

The work was planned to fit a 30-day launch window, with the scope kept focused on what was needed to open the business.
