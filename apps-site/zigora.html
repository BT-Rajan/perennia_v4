The problem

Studying without a plan or a way to get unstuck

Students at school and university often study without a clear plan for what to cover and in what order. When they hit a question they cannot answer, they fall back on search results, notes from friends or waiting for the next lesson. Practice is usually limited to whatever a textbook or teacher happens to provide, so it is hard to know whether they are ready.

Schools and families want something that supports the learner at every step, not a single tool used once before an exam.

How we solved it

One assistant that plans, explains and tests

Zigora starts with the student. They tell it where and what they study, and it prepares a learning plan around that. As they work through the plan, the assistant stays with them: a question can be typed, spoken or sent as a photo of the problem on the page.

  • Personalised learning plans based on the student's curriculum and goals.
  • Question handling by text, voice or picture, so the student can ask in the way that suits them.
  • System-generated multiple-choice practice tests to check understanding before an exam.

Individual students are already signing up, and schools are evaluating the product and customising it to their specific needs.
