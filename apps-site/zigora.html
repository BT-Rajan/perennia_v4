Zigora Intelligence
Case study · Education

One place for a student to plan what to study, get unstuck, and check they are ready.

Zigora Intelligence is an AI learning platform for students from high school through to graduation. It turns what and where a student studies into a personal learning plan, and stays with them as they work through it.

Sector
Education
Learners
High school to graduation
Status
Launched October 2026
Ask by
Text, voice or picture

The challenge

Most students study without a clear plan for what to cover and in what order. When they reach a question they cannot answer, they fall back on search results, notes from friends, or waiting for the next lesson. Practice is limited to whatever a textbook or teacher happens to provide, so it is hard for a student to know whether they are actually ready.

Students, and the schools and families behind them, needed support at every step of learning, not a single tool opened the night before an exam.

The approach

We designed Zigora around the student's own context. It begins by asking where and what they study, so the plan it produces reflects their curriculum and goals rather than a generic syllabus. From there, three capabilities work together in one assistant.

The solution: plan, ask, practise

  1. Profile
  2. Learning plan
  3. Questions
  4. Practice tests
  5. Exam readiness
  • Personal learning plans — built from the student's curriculum, institution and goals, so they always know what to study next.
  • Questions in any form — a question can be typed, spoken, or sent as a photo of the problem on the page, whichever is quickest for the student.
  • Practice on demand — the system generates multiple-choice tests so students can check their understanding whenever they choose.

The outcome

Zigora launched in October 2026. Individual students are already signing up, and schools are evaluating the platform and tailoring it to their own needs.

  • Structured study — learners follow a plan instead of deciding what to do each day.
  • Help at the moment it is needed — no waiting for the next class to clear a doubt.
  • Measurable readiness — practice tests show where a student stands before the exam does.
  • Adaptable for institutions — schools can shape the product around their programmes.

What it demonstrates

  • AI applied to a specific, everyday problem rather than as a general-purpose chat tool
  • Multi-modal input — text, voice and images — in one consistent experience
  • A consumer product designed to be customised for institutional customers
