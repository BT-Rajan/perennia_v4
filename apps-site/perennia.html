Perennia CMS Portal
Case study · Product

Our own zero-code content platform — the system that runs perennia.org.

The Perennia CMS Portal is the content platform behind perennia.org. We built it for ourselves first, so that our website could be run by the people responsible for its content rather than by developers.

Type
Perennia product
Status
In use on perennia.org; Phase 2
Approach
Zero-code
Used for
Pages, content and site settings

The challenge

For many businesses, every change to the website — a new page, an updated service description, a revised contact detail — becomes a request to a developer and a wait for the next release. The people who own the message are not the people who can publish it.

The approach

We applied the same principle we use for client systems: build around the people who do the work. Content and site settings are managed through an administration portal, and the public site reads them directly, so changes do not require code or a rebuild.

The solution

  • Zero-code publishing — pages and their content are created and edited in the admin portal.
  • Configurable site settings — the look of the site and its navigation are managed as settings, not code.
  • Proven in production — perennia.org runs on the platform today.

Status

The platform is in daily use on perennia.org. Making it available to clients is part of our Phase 2 roadmap. If you would like to be among the first to use it, we would be glad to talk.
