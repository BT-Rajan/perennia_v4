# Custom Software Development in Kuwait

> Software built around how your business actually works — not a template your team has to work around.

Perennia is a Kuwait-based software development company. We design and build custom business software — ERP systems, practice and project management platforms, customer portals, booking systems and mobile apps — for growing businesses in Kuwait and across the GCC, from the first conversation through to support in production.

## Why custom software

Off-the-shelf packages are built to serve every business, so most businesses end up bending their processes to fit them. Our client JDK, a cement manufacturer, found its existing ERP (Odoo) unnecessarily complicated and rigid for day-to-day operations. We replaced it with a simpler system built around the plant's own workflow, delivered in 30 days.

Custom software makes sense when your operation has rules, approvals or workflows a generic product can't follow, when information is re-keyed between separate tools, or when you need a system your team will actually use.

## What we build

- **ERP and business systems** — sales, procurement, inventory, production, delivery and payments in one connected workflow, with the approvals and rules your business runs on.
- **Practice and project management** — clients, engagements, tasks, deadlines, documents and billing in one place.
- **Customer portals and platforms** — web platforms with online payment gateways, built to launch quickly and grow.
- **Booking and scheduling systems** — self-service booking with live availability, confirmations, rescheduling and cancellation.
- **Mobile and field apps** — installable Arabic/English apps that let teams work from customer sites and keep managers informed.
- **Integrations and automation** — connecting your CRM, inbox and internal tools so work moves forward without manual chasing.

## Built for Kuwait and the GCC

- **Arabic and English**, with proper right-to-left layouts.
- **Local conventions** built in — Kuwait time, amounts in KWD to three decimals and DD-MM-YYYY dates, as in the JDK Factory ERP.
- **Meetings in person in Kuwait or virtually**, whichever suits you.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does custom software development cost in Kuwait?

It depends on the scope: the processes involved, the number of users and roles, and the integrations needed. We agree scope and investment once we understand your requirements, after the discovery meeting. No solution or price is committed in that first meeting.

### How long does it take to build custom software?

Focused systems can be delivered quickly: the JDK Factory ERP and the BBK Game Portal each took 30 days, and the Consultancy Management System went live in 75 days. We plan the timeline with you once the scope is clear.

### Do you build software in Arabic?

Yes. We build bilingual Arabic and English systems with right-to-left layouts, and this website works the same way.

### Do we own and can we maintain the software?

Every system comes with clean handover documentation, so your team can maintain and extend it long after delivery.

### Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. After launch we stay available for monitoring, improvement and scaling, on a schedule that matches your needs.
