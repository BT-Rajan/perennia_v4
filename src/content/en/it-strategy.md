# IT Strategy and Technology Consulting in Kuwait

> Make better technology decisions — before you commit budget to them.

Perennia's IT strategy and advisory work helps businesses in Kuwait and the GCC decide what technology they need, in what order, and at what cost. We map how your business really works, identify where technology — AI or otherwise — can create genuine value, and give you a realistic plan with costs and timelines.

## When businesses come to us

- **Starting digital** — moving from spreadsheets, paper and disconnected tools to systems the business can run on.
- **Making AI practical** — separating the AI opportunities that will pay off from the ones that won't.
- **Scaling technology** — systems that worked at one size have become complicated, rigid or expensive as the business grew.

## What an IT strategy engagement covers

- **Your processes as they really run** — how work flows between people, departments and tools, and where it gets stuck.
- **Your current systems** — what's working, what's duplicated, and where information is re-keyed.
- **Build, buy or replace** — whether an existing product fits, or whether custom software would serve you better. Our client JDK replaced a rigid, complicated ERP with a simpler system built around its own workflow.
- **AI readiness** — where AI can help today, and what needs to be in place first.
- **A realistic roadmap** — priorities, scope, costs and timelines you can plan and budget around.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an IT strategy?

A clear, prioritised plan for the technology your business needs: which processes to improve, which systems to build, buy or replace, where AI fits, and the costs and timelines involved.

### How do we start?

With a 30-minute discovery meeting. We look at your business, your current process or problem, the outcome you want, whether technology can help, and what the sensible next step is. It's a working conversation, not a sales pitch.

### Is IT strategy only for large organisations?

No. We work mainly with SMEs in Kuwait and the GCC, typically around 100 to 500 people, at whatever stage your business is at.

### Do we have to use Perennia to implement the plan?

No. You can work with us as an advisor only, or have us implement part or all of the plan. Scope and investment are agreed once we understand your requirements.
